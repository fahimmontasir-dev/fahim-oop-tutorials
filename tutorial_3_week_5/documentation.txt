
Student Information System - Week 5

2.1 Why are the variables declared as private?

Variables are declared as private to protect student information from being accessed or modified directly outside the Student class. This helps maintain data security and encapsulation.

2.2 What happens if the variables are declared public?

If the variables are public, other classes can directly access and modify them. This reduces data protection and can lead to unwanted changes.

2.3 Why do we use getters and setters?

Getters allow other classes to retrieve private information, while setters allow controlled modification of private variables. They support encapsulation and help manage access to data.
